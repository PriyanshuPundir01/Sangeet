Sangeet: Your Melodious Companion is a simple, easy-to-use music and video web app that brings the soulful sounds of Garhwali music right to you. From traditional folk songs that echo the spirit of the hills to fresh new beats that keep you moving, Sangeet celebrates the music of Uttarakhand. Enjoy handpicked playlists, regional videos, and a beautiful design that makes listening fun and effortless. Whether you're at home or far away, Sangeet helps you stay connected to your culture through music you love.
